# Synthetic Revenue Cycle Operations Playbook
This is fictional sample material, not an actual healthcare policy.

## High-risk claim escalation
Claims with a high denial risk should be reviewed by the revenue integrity specialist within two business days. If supporting documentation is missing, the specialist contacts the responsible coding team. Unresolved claims are escalated to the RCM operations manager after five business days.

## Weekly reporting
The operations dashboard summarizes total claims, denial rate, days in accounts receivable, and appeal turnaround time. The reporting analyst reviews changes weekly and documents unusual movements.

## Data privacy
Only synthetic or authorized de-identified datasets may be used in demonstration environments. Protected health information must not be uploaded to public portfolio tools.
